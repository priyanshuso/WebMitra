PRICING QUESTIONS

Everything you should know before starting.

Still have questions about payments, maintenance or additional pages?

What are the ongoing costs? +

Maintenance is ₹499 per month and you can stop any time. Domain renewal is roughly ₹800–₹1,200 a year and hosting ₹2,000–₹4,000 a year, both billed at what the provider charges.

Is there a payment schedule? +

Half at the start, half on the day the site goes live. No payment is due between the consultation and your approval of the design.

What if I need more pages later? +

Additional pages are ₹800 each on Starter and Growth. On the Institutional plan core pages are already unlimited.

Do you offer refunds? +

If you don't approve the design preview in step two, we refund the advance in full. After development starts, the advance covers work already done.
