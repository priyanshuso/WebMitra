STRAIGHT ANSWERS

Questions clients usually ask.

Still wondering how working with WebMitra actually works? Here are some straightforward answers.

Do I own the website after you build it?

Yes. The domain is registered in your name, the hosting account is yours, and the required login details are handed over to you.

What if I don't have photos or content ready?

We can start with a structure and placeholders and replace them as your content becomes available. For many local businesses, clear phone photos are enough to get started.

Can you update my existing website?

Yes, depending on how the existing website is built. We'll review it first and tell you whether updating it makes sense or whether rebuilding would be more practical.

Do you work with clients outside Bhopal?

Yes. Most communication and project work can happen remotely through calls, messages and online collaboration.
