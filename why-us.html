Straight answers

Things clients ask before signing

Do I own the website after you build it?

Yes, completely. The domain is registered in your name, the hosting account is yours, and we hand over every login. If you ever want to move to another developer, nothing holds you back.

What if I don't have photos or content ready?

We start with placeholder text and a structure, then swap in your material as it arrives. For shops we often just need clear phone photos of your products - no studio shoot required.

Can you update my existing website instead?

Sometimes. If it's built on a platform we can work with, we'll quote for a redesign. If it's badly built, rebuilding is usually cheaper than patching, and we'll say so.

Do you work with clients outside Bhopal?

Yes. Most of the work happens over calls and WhatsApp, so location doesn't change the price or the timeline.
